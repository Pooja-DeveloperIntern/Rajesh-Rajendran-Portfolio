About me

Engineer. Simulator. Launch partner.

I’m a manufacturing and automotive engineer with more than ten years across new product introduction, virtual manufacturing, dimensional engineering and plant launch.

At Ashok Leyland I run DMU and Design for Assembly studies in DELMIA. Before that, at Mahindra Research Valley, I defined gap and flush specifications for ICE and EV programmes. At Renault Nissan I spent seven years from greenfield plant setup to Process Simulate studies, supporting launches in India and abroad.

I work between design and the shop floor, checking that a vehicle can be assembled, that its panels fit and that cycle times hold up before production begins.
